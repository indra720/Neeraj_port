THE CROWN JEWEL

Flagship 200-Bigha Destination Resort

The cornerstone of Joy Hospitality Group is its flagship 200-bigha integrated destination resort in Rajasthan. Conceived to harmonize royal Rajasthani sandstone craftsmanship with high-adrenaline adventure sports and restorative eco-tourism, this project represents approximately 8 years of dedicated civil engineering and master planning.

Currently standing at approximately 70% completion as reported in the profile, the completed development carries an estimated projected value of ₹300+ Crore.

FINANCIAL & STRUCTURAL INNOVATION

Debenture-Based Investment Model

A key structural hallmark of Joy Hospitality Group is its integrated concept uniting Real Estate and Hospitality on a single operational platform. To facilitate structured institutional participation and private investor alignment, a debenture-based investment model was incorporated as part of the project framework.

This model provides security, transparency, and participation in the long-term capital appreciation of regional land banks coupled with operating hospitality cash flows.

NATIONAL EXPANSION

Pan-India Vision: 10+ Resort Destinations

The long-term roadmap envisions the establishment of 10+ resort destinations across key growth states in India. Rather than conventional hotels or static resorts, each location is master-planned as a living ecosystem:

  • Integrated Hospitality + Plotted Real Estate communities.
  • Adventure sports grounds, equestrian arenas, and natural lakes.
  • Celebration banqueting lawns catering to destination weddings and corporate conferences.
  • Local employment generation and revitalization of regional handicraft traditions.
SPORTS & CULTURE

Women's Kabaddi League

Neeraj Sharma has actively supported youth athletics and traditional Indian sports. Joy Group backed a Women's Kabaddi League franchise, whose historic first season was hosted in Dubai in 2023.

The next phase is planned for India under the Women's Kabaddi Premier League concept, promoting gender inclusivity and international sports diplomacy.
