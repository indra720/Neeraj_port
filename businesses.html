The 11 Business Verticals

"Multiple Sectors. One Integrated Vision." Comprehensive capabilities spanning heritage craftsmanship, global trade, and master-planned regional infrastructure.

VERTICAL 01

Real Estate Development

Land development & integrated projects

Focuses on strategic regional land acquisition, plotted residential development, and commercial layouts in high-growth corridors of Rajasthan. Prioritizes transparent titling, sustainable zoning, and civil durability.

Enquire About Real Estate →
VERTICAL 02

Hospitality

Hotels, resorts & leisure experiences

Creating immersive guest environments that celebrate indigenous culinary traditions, royal Rajasthani courtesy, and rejuvenating wellness retreats in pristine regional settings.

Explore Joy Hospitality →
VERTICAL 03

Resort Development

Destination projects & experiential stays

Spearheading mega-scale experiential resorts. Anchored by the 200-bigha flagship in Rajasthan (~70% complete, ₹300+ Cr projected value), integrating adventure sports, equestrian trails, and royal banquet lawns.

View Flagship Resort Details →
VERTICAL 04

Natural Stone

Processing, supply & global trade

The foundational pillar since 1998. Direct quarrying and precision dressing of Karauli red, pink, and beige sandstone, calibrated marble, and structural granite for landmark projects worldwide.

Direct Stone Sourcing →
VERTICAL 05

Temple Construction

Heritage, architecture & spiritual projects

Preserving centuries-old Vedic Shilpa Shastra traditions. Dedicated guilds of generational Rajasthani master carvers sculpt ornate shikharas, mandapas, and monolithic sanctums for sacred pilgrimage complexes.

Temple Architecture Enquiries →
VERTICAL 06

Handicrafts

Traditional crafts & global markets

Empowering rural artisanal families by connecting authentic Rajasthani stone sculptures, jharokhas, marble fountains, and brass decorative elements directly with global collectors and architects.

Curated Crafts Enquiries →
VERTICAL 07

Import & Export

Global trade & supply chain

Over 20 years of maritime and overland freight coordination linking Arabian Gulf ports (Jebel Ali, Sharjah) with Indian shipping lanes (Mundra, Kandla) for on-time delivery of building commodities.

View Trade Footprint →
VERTICAL 08

Metal Trading

Sourcing, trading & distribution

Wholesale procurement and commercial trading of structural steel, aluminum profiles, and industrial construction metals servicing major civil contractors across the Middle East and India.

Metal Trade Alliances →
VERTICAL 09

Landscaping

Green spaces & outdoor development

Uniting botanical horticulture with stone hardscaping. Designing natural amphitheaters, cascading stone fountains, paved promenades, and indigenous drought-tolerant parks.

Landscape Architecture →
VERTICAL 10

Sports & Entertainment

Recreation, events & lifestyle projects

Backing indigenous sports and female athletic empowerment through the Women's Kabaddi League franchise (Dubai 2023 international season), youth tournaments, and outdoor adventure courses.

Sports Sponsorship Dialogue →
VERTICAL 11

Large-Scale Development Projects

Integrated townships, mixed-use & national-level projects

Master-planning multi-hectare destinations encompassing civic utilities, entertainment corridors, and regional economic zones that catalyze multi-generational prosperity.
