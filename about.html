THE FOUNDATIONAL YEARS

Where Craftsmanship Meets Vision

Born in Karauli District, Hindaun City, Rajasthan, Neeraj Sharma was raised in an environment saturated with architectural heritage. Karauli is globally celebrated for its unique pink and red sandstone—materials that have formed the sacred fabric of historic forts, palaces, and ancient Hindu shrines across the Indian subcontinent.

Surrounded by master stonemasons and carvers, he developed an instinctive understanding of stone extraction, geological texture, and traditional artisan craftsmanship. Unlike conventional tradesmen, he recognized early on that while Rajasthan’s craftsmanship was timeless, its commercial reach and industrial precision required modernization and global linkages.

1998 INDEPENDENT INCEPTION

Pioneering Natural Stone & Sacred Architecture

In 1998, Neeraj began his independent entrepreneurial career in natural stone processing and sacred temple architecture. By assembling dedicated teams of skilled artisans and introducing structured project delivery, he established supply networks across India and laid the cornerstone for institutional-scale construction.

INTERNATIONAL LEADERSHIP

The Middle East Chapter (2004–2016)

In the early 2000s, recognizing the exponential infrastructure boom in the Arabian Gulf, Neeraj expanded his business horizon to Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Over a decade of rigorous international operations, he established Al Mendal Granite LLC in Sharjah, managing modern fabrication yards equipped with advanced waterjet cutting and automated profiling machinery.

His companies delivered specialized dimensional stone, marble claddings, and custom architectural features to ultra-luxury villas and commercial landmarks on Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, and commercial towers across Dubai, Sharjah, Bahrain, and Riyadh. This decade solidified his commitment to international tolerance standards, precision logistics, and cross-border commercial execution.

RETURN & TRANSFORMATION

Consolidating Joy Hospitality Group

Armed with deep international experience and a strong financial foundation, Neeraj returned to India with an ambition to build integrated regional destinations. He founded Joy Hospitality Group and Joy Adventure Group, channeling his knowledge of stonecraft, engineering, and master planning into modern resort hospitality and experiential tourism.

Today, his flagship project—a sprawling 200 Bigha integrated destination resort in Rajasthan—combines luxury cottage accommodations, adventure theme parks, equestrian facilities, cultural open-air amphitheatres, and sustainable green farming. It stands as a living demonstration of his guiding philosophy: using real estate and hospitality as engines of grassroots employment and heritage preservation.

GRASSROOTS IMPACT

Empowering Youth & Rural Sports

Believing that true business leadership is measured by community empowerment, Neeraj is an active patron of indigenous sports. He established a dedicated Kabaddi Academy and sponsors women's sports tournaments across Rajasthan, creating disciplined pathways for rural youth into regional and national athletics.

PHILOSOPHY

The Four Pillars of Leadership

The enduring values that guide every venture, development, and strategic partnership.

People First

Prioritizing direct employment for local artisans, masons, and hospitality teams, ensuring sustainable livelihoods and community prosperity.

Sacred Heritage

Honoring the ancient traditions of Rajasthani sandstone and temple architecture while applying modern engineering and safety standards.

Global Standards

Instilling international quality controls, precision fabrication, and reliable delivery practices honed across a decade in the Middle East.

Sustainable Future

Designing eco-conscious developments with native landscaping, rainwater retention, and integrated green spaces for long-term ecological balance.
