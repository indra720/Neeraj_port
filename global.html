INTERNATIONAL TRADE & GCC OPERATIONS

From India to the Middle East

A decade of high-level commerce, warehouse logistics in Sharjah under "Al Mendal Granite", and precision material supply across the Arabian Gulf.

CROSS-BORDER COMMERCE

The Middle East Epoch (2004 – 2016)

In the mid-2000s, Neeraj Sharma established his commercial presence in Dubai and the Northern Emirates, anticipating the explosive infrastructure growth of the Gulf. Under the corporate identity "Al Mendal Granite" in Sharjah, he established major warehouse operations and automated processing yards that served the UAE, Bahrain, and Saudi Arabia for over a decade.

His business activities established direct supply pipelines connecting Indian natural sandstone and granite clusters directly with high-profile commercial developments, five-star resorts, and luxury residential projects across Dubai, Sharjah, Manama (Bahrain), and Riyadh.

"Working across the UAE and GCC taught me that world-class execution demands precision tolerances, absolute adherence to schedules, and uncompromising supply chains. Those principles now drive every venture we build in India."

CORE CAPABILITIES

Four Pillars of GCC Commerce

Al Mendal Granite (Sharjah)

Fully operational fabrication plant and warehouse yard in Sharjah, specialized in custom dimensional granite cut-to-size, waterjet profiling, and dry-hang façade systems.

Dubai Landmark Supply

Direct supplier of premium sandstone, honed paving, and ornamental columns for luxury private estates on Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, and civic park corridors.

Bahrain & Saudi Distribution

Coordinated multimodal maritime shipments from Indian ports directly into Port Khalifa (Bahrain) and Dammam (Saudi Arabia), supporting large commercial building contracts.

Metal & Structural Trading

Diversified into regional metal trading, structural steel fittings, and architectural ironwork, strengthening integration with main contractors and engineering developers.
