CHRONOLOGICAL MILESTONES

The Entrepreneurial Journey

From Rajasthan's sacred stone quarries to Dubai's commercial trade hubs and back to build visionary Indian destinations.

HERITAGE ORIGINS

Early Life & Family Roots

Neeraj Sharma was born in Karauli District, Hindaun City, Rajasthan, India. He was raised in a family deeply associated with natural stone, craftsmanship, and construction, which laid the bedrock for his entrepreneurial discipline, material reverence, and aesthetic sensibility.

Surrounded by master carvers chiseling red and pink sandstone according to classical Shilpa Shastra canons, he learned early that architectural endurance is forged through patience, honesty, and uncompromising quality.

Rooted in Heritage, Driven by Opportunities
1998

Entrepreneurial Beginnings

Natural Stone Export & Temple Construction

Started his independent entrepreneurial journey in Natural Stone Export and Temple Construction in Rajasthan. With a strong commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and long-term relationships, he built a solid foundation in the industry.

Key Focus: Direct quarry alliances, hand-carved sandstone sanctums, and sacred architectural geometry.
2003

International Expansion to Dubai

UAE, Sharjah & The Middle East

Moved to Dubai, UAE, beginning a major international expansion phase. Established a warehouse and business operations in Sharjah under the enterprise name "Al Mendal Granite", continuing operations until approximately 2013.

Worked on several large-scale construction and stone-related projects across Dubai, Bahrain, Riyadh, and Saudi Arabia. Expanded beyond natural stone into metal import and trading, building strong relationships with international clients, contractors, and suppliers.

2013

Return to India — A Strategic New Beginning

Consolidation & Domestic Sourcing

Returned to India in 2013, marking a new chapter. Expanded the existing Import-Export business in the Indian market, utilizing international experience and relationships to enter new sectors and prepare for high-impact regional developments.

"From international experience to a diversified enterprise in India."

2014+

Diversification: Real Estate & Hospitality

Launch of Joy Adventure Group

Expanded into Real Estate and Hospitality; launched the Joy Adventure Group and Joy Hospitality platform. Acquired and commenced master development of the 200-bigha flagship resort project in Rajasthan, reported at approximately 70% completion with an estimated value of ₹300+ Crore.

Formulated an ambitious Pan-India resort roadmap covering 10+ resort destinations. Pioneered sports entertainment initiatives, including supporting the historic Dubai season of the Women's Kabaddi League in 2023.

Next

Pan-India Multi-Sector Ecosystem

Hospitality • Real Estate • Leisure • Investment

Active expansion toward establishing a nationally recognized Pan-India Real Estate and Hospitality Group, combining Real Estate, Hospitality, Leisure, Entertainment, and debenture-based investment into a single unified ecosystem.
