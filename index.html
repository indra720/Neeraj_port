ABOUT NEERAJ SHARMA

Where Craftsmanship Meets Enterprise

Born in Karauli District, Hindaun City, Rajasthan, Neeraj Sharma was raised in a family deeply associated with natural stone, sacred craftsmanship, and construction. This heritage laid the foundation for his entrepreneurial journey.

In 1998, he commenced his independent path in natural stone export and temple construction. Over the subsequent two and a half decades, he expanded his vision from Rajasthan's quarries to international operations in Dubai and the wider Middle East, eventually returning to India to spearhead large-scale real estate, resort development, and sports initiatives.

CHRONOLOGICAL MILESTONES

A Quarter Century of Enterprise

From indigenous natural stone export to international trade in the Arabian Gulf and modern integrated hospitality in India.

1998

Entrepreneurial Beginnings

Started his entrepreneurial venture in Natural Stone Export and Temple Construction in Rajasthan. Built on uncompromising commitment to craftsmanship, stone quality, and long-term business integrity.

2003

International Expansion (Dubai & Sharjah)

Relocated to Dubai, UAE, initiating a decade-long international expansion. Established operations and a warehouse in Sharjah under "Al Mendal Granite" (operating until ~2013). Expanded into metal trading and large-scale Gulf construction supply.

2013

Return to India & Consolidation

Returned to India to establish a new beginning. Scaled the existing import-export network and channeled international commercial relationships into domestic growth opportunities.

2014+

Real Estate & Joy Adventure Group

Expanded into Real Estate and Hospitality by launching Joy Adventure Group / Joy Hospitality Group. Initiated the 200-bigha flagship destination resort and formulated a national roadmap.

Next

Pan-India Integrated Destinations

Scaling an envisioned pipeline of 10+ resort destinations across India, combining hospitality, sports, entertainment, and debenture-based investment ecosystems.
