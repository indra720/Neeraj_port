GLOBAL FOOTPRINT & GCC OPERATIONS

From India to the Middle East

A decade of high-level commerce, warehouse logistics in Sharjah under "Al Mendal Granite", and material supply across the Arabian Gulf.

CROSS-BORDER COMMERCE

The Middle East Epoch (2003 – 2013)

In 2003, Neeraj Sharma moved to Dubai, UAE, beginning a major international expansion phase. Seizing the immense infrastructure growth in the region, he established a commercial warehouse and operational base in Sharjah under the trade name "Al Mendal Granite", which operated actively until approximately 2013.

His business activities bridged Indian stone quarry clusters with prestigious commercial developments across Dubai, Sharjah, the Kingdom of Bahrain, Riyadh, and Saudi Arabia. He built enduring alliances with international clients, civil contractors, structural engineers, and government project consultants.

"From India to the Middle East — Creating Global Opportunities."

DIVERSIFIED COMMODITIES

Import-Export & Metal Trading Expansion

Recognizing the vast commodity requirements of GCC mega-projects, Neeraj Sharma expanded beyond natural stone into metal import and industrial trading. He developed capabilities in international sourcing, marine logistics, customs clearance, and multi-modal supply chains.

This resilient network connecting Middle Eastern ports (Jebel Ali, Sharjah) with Indian maritime corridors (Mundra, Kandla) created a durable commercial foundation that proved invaluable upon his strategic return to India in 2013.
